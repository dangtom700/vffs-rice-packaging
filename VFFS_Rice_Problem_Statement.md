## VFFS 1 kg Block-Brick Rice Packaging Machine

| | |
|---|---|

| **Date** | 2026-09-08 |

---

## 1. Background

In the retail rice category the competitive sweet spot for a Vertical Form-Fill-Seal (VFFS) line is the **1–5 kg bulk pack**, where the range of products and pack designs is widest and most appealing. Within that band, a **1 kg firm-fill "block brick"** — a block-bottom (box-bottom), quad-seal bag whose contents are settled and shaped into a dense, square, self-standing rectangular brick at atmospheric pressure — is compact, stackable, easy to carry in one hand, and well suited to high-barrier films that preserve shelf life. Achieving this pack demands precise gravimetric dosing, fast bag formation, a consistently dense and square brick, and airtight seals to prevent moisture ingress. Packaging lines routinely trade high volumetric output against gravimetric dosing accuracy, producing either costly product giveaway or reduced line throughput.

## 2. Problem Statement

Current high-speed packaging operations struggle to maximize throughput while maintaining tight target-weight tolerances for dry parboiled / instant rice **and** forming a dense, dimensionally consistent, retail-ready brick, resulting in financial loss from overfilling, out-of-square bricks, or bottlenecks in the weighing-settling-sealing cycle.

Specifically, single-head or volumetric feeding systems cannot consistently achieve the target mass of **1000 g** within the strict operational band of **980 g to 1020 g (±2.0 % tolerance)** without throttling line speed below optimal rates. Forming a firm block brick adds a settling/densification and shaping step to the cycle, and the block-bottom format introduces additional seams (bottom gusset plus back and top seals). The combination of line velocity and light, free-falling dry grains increases the risk of grain entrapment across this longer seal set, compromising seal integrity and causing downtime, while inconsistent settling produces soft or out-of-square bricks that will not stack or merchandise cleanly.

## 3. Engineering Objectives & Constraints

**Primary Objective** — Design, integrate, and optimize a VFFS machine paired with a Multi-Head Weigher (MHW) to produce **1 kg firm-fill block-bottom (quad-seal) brick bags** at maximum sustainable speed, targeting an operational baseline of **≥ 25 bags per minute (bpm)** with an engineering stretch goal of **40+ bpm**. (Block-bottom forming plus a settling/shaping step makes this range lower than a plain 500 g pillow bag; > 45 bpm implies a continuous-motion / twin-former architecture.)

**Dosing Precision** — Maintain 100 % of finished 1000 g bricks strictly within the 980–1020 g range, targeting a process capability index (Cpk) **≥ 1.33** and a mean fill weight closer to **1002 g** to minimize product giveaway.

**Product Scope** — Dry parboiled / instant rice grains (free-flowing, low-moisture, bulk density ≈ **0.75–0.85 g/cm³**).

**Brick Form & Seal Integrity** — Densify the metered charge by vibratory settling / de-aeration (with optional tamping) and shape it with a block-bottom former and shaping frame into a **firm, square, self-standing brick** of consistent dimensions and density; guarantee **zero grain entrapment** across the bottom-gusset, back, and top seals; achieve airtight, moisture-barrier, retail-ready bricks.

**Operational Scope** — Covers film unwind, active web tracking, block-bottom tube forming, multi-head weigh discharge, settling / densification / brick shaping, film advancing, vertical (back) / bottom-gusset / horizontal (top) thermal sealing, cutting, and discharge onto downstream conveyors. *(Cartoning excluded.)*
